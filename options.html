About

PasteGuard detects and blocks ClickFix-style attacks that place dangerous commands on your clipboard. All detection is performed locally — no data is sent externally.

Limitations: PasteGuard cannot disable Win+R or prevent pasting into external apps. Browser extensions do not have OS-level keyboard control. PasteGuard prevents dangerous clipboard content from being placed on the clipboard and warns users before they paste into risky contexts.
